Requires Node 18+ (Node 20+ recommended).
Run: node --test ai-business-os/marketing-agency/auto-research-runner/deep-research.test.js
Run: node ai-business-os/marketing-agency/auto-research-runner/deep-research.js
Input: local-research-output/latest-market-intelligence.json
Output: local-research-output/latest-deep-research.json
This is an independent read-only research step. No existing files are overwritten except its own report.
Source pages may be unavailable, particularly Google News RSS links. Those will be reported as SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE; no invented facts.
Only page metadata and Product JSON-LD are extracted. No image/video analysis or social network scraping.
